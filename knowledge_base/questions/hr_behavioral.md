# HR and behavioral interview questions

## Introduction and motivation
Easy: Tell me about yourself. (Reported at almost every company: Tata Power, L&T Power, Polycab, NTPC)
Easy: Tell us about your family background. (Reported: Adani Group, Tata Power, Schneider)
Easy: Why are you interested in this role?
Easy: Where do you see yourself in five years? (Reported: Adani Group, Schneider, Havells)
Good answers cover: a 60-90 second story (present, past, future), relevant highlights from projects or experience, genuine and specific motivation connected to the role.

## Strengths, weaknesses and self-awareness
Easy: What are your strengths? (Reported: Tata Power, BHEL, Schneider, GE Vernova)
Intermediate: What is your biggest weakness?
Hard: Why should we hire you? (Reported: Adani Group, Schneider)
Good answers cover: evidence instead of adjectives, a real (not disguised) weakness with concrete improvement steps.

## Teamwork and conflict
Intermediate: Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate. What did you do?
Intermediate: Describe a time you helped a team succeed under pressure.
Good answers cover: STAR structure, listening and understanding the other view, focusing on the problem not the person, a constructive outcome.

## Challenges and failure
Intermediate: Tell me about a project that did not go as planned.
Hard: Tell me about a time you failed. What did you learn?
Hard: Describe the hardest technical problem you solved.
Intermediate: Tell me about a time you took the lead on a problem. (Reported: Hitachi Energy)
Good answers cover: ownership without blaming others, what was learned, what would be done differently.

## Learning and adaptability
Easy: How do you learn something new quickly? Give a recent example.
Good answers cover: a concrete learning method, building something small, measurable progress.

## Final-year project and internship deep dive (core electrical)
Intermediate: Explain your final-year project. (Reported at nearly every core company: Tata Power, Adani, Torrent Power, NHPC, POWERGRID, L&T, Siemens Energy, Schneider)
Intermediate: (follow-up) What exactly did you do yourself in the project?
Hard: What was the hardest problem in your project, and how did you solve it?
Easy: What did you learn in your internship or plant training?
Good answers cover: a drawable diagram (SLD, block diagram), your own contribution separated from the team's, the numbers you measured or calculated, one problem you debugged, what you would improve; several candidates report the whole technical round came from the internship, so know every term on your training report.

## Core-company and PSU HR questions
Easy: Why do you want to join our company? (Reported: Adani, Tata Power, L&T, BHEL, Schneider, POWERGRID, Hitachi Energy)
Easy: What do you know about our company? (Reported: Adani Group, L&T)
Easy: Are you willing to relocate anywhere in India? (Reported: Adani, L&T, ABB, Schneider)
Easy: Would you prefer office work or site work? (Reported: L&T)
Easy: Why did you choose electrical engineering? (Reported: L&T, GE Vernova)
Easy: Do you plan to go for higher studies? (Reported: Tata Power, L&T)
Intermediate: Tell us about your GATE score. (Reported: POWERGRID)
Hard: Why do you want a core job and not an IT job?
Hard: This PSU has a service bond. Are you comfortable with it?
Hard: Speak for two minutes on a topic you get 30 seconds to prepare. (Reported: L&T)
Good answers cover: specific company knowledge (products, recent projects, plants, subsidiaries), a genuine reason for core work tied to a subject or project you enjoyed, a clear yes on relocation and site work when you mean it, honest higher-study plans, calm structure in an extempore (one point, an example, a conclusion).

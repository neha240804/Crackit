# Managerial and leadership interview questions

## Leading people
Mid: Tell me about a time you mentored someone. How did you measure their growth?
Senior: How do you handle a consistently under-performing team member?
Good answers cover: regular one-to-ones, clear expectations, specific feedback, support plans, fairness and documentation.

## Prioritisation and decision making
Mid: You have three urgent requests and capacity for one. How do you decide?
Senior: Describe a difficult decision you made with incomplete information.
Good answers cover: impact vs effort, stakeholder alignment, explicit trade-offs, revisiting decisions with new data.

## Stakeholder management
Mid: How do you handle a stakeholder who keeps changing requirements?
Good answers cover: understanding the underlying goal, written agreements, change impact made visible, saying no constructively.

## Delivery and ownership
Mid: Tell me about a time a deadline was at risk. What did you do?
Good answers cover: early communication, scope negotiation, unblocking the team, lessons for planning.

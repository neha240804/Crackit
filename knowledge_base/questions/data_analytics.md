# Data analyst and data science interview questions

## SQL for analysis
Fresher: What is the difference between WHERE and HAVING?
Mid: Explain window functions with a use case such as running totals or ranking.
Good answers cover: aggregation order of execution, GROUP BY, joins and their pitfalls (duplicates), ROW_NUMBER, RANK, LAG/LEAD.

## Statistics
Fresher: Explain mean, median and when the median is more useful.
Mid: What is a p-value and what is a common misunderstanding of it?
Mid: How would you design and evaluate an A/B test?
Good answers cover: skewed distributions and outliers, hypothesis testing, significance vs practical effect size, sample size, randomisation, peeking problems.

## Data cleaning and exploration
Fresher: Walk me through how you explore a new dataset.
Good answers cover: shape and types, missing values, duplicates, distributions, outliers, sanity checks against business knowledge.

## Communicating insights
Mid: Tell me how you would present a finding to a non-technical stakeholder.
Good answers cover: lead with the recommendation, one clear chart, quantify impact, state limitations.

## Python for data
Fresher: How do you handle missing data in pandas?
Mid: How would you speed up slow pandas code?
Good answers cover: isna/fillna/dropna, vectorisation over loops, appropriate dtypes, chunking large files.

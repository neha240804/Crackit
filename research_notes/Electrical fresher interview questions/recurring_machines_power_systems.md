# Recurring Electrical Machines and Power Systems questions for Indian EE freshers (cross-company frequency)

> **Read this first: access limits and evidence quality.** About 19 search and fetch calls were made (November 2026).
> - **Blocked (HTTP 403):** Glassdoor (every domain tried), AmbitionBox (profile pages) and Quora. Evidence from those sites comes only from **search-result snippets**. The candidate's own review text could not be read, so role, date and whether the candidate was a fresher in India are often unconfirmed.
> - **Not found:** No Reddit thread with first-person core-EE interview questions turned up in any search, so Reddit gave **zero evidence**.
> - **Fetched and verified:** Two GeeksforGeeks pages. One is a real EE account (Emami GET 2023). The other (Adani GET) turned out to be an ECE/IT interview with no machines or power-systems questions.
> - **What dominates:** Most search results are question banks (electricalvolt, eeemadeeasy, hitbullseye, Scribd, Skill-Lync). They are **not** evidence that a question was asked.
>
> **Bottom line:** The evidence shows **topic-level** recurrence (transformers and induction motors come up at many companies). It does **not** show that any **single specific question** was reported by 3 or more independent candidates. Do not mark any specific question "most frequently reported" from these notes alone.

## Which of these questions are reported as actually asked by more than one independent candidate? List each source.

### Takeaway
At the **topic** level, transformers and induction motors are reported in candidate accounts from at least 7 separate employers: L&T Power, Tata Power, Siemens Energy, ReNew, UPL, Emami and IMEG. Protection and relays are reported at 3 (UPL, IMEG, Hitachi Energy). At the **specific-question** level, only these have 2 independent reports, both partly snippet-based:
- induction motor working / types / classification
- transformer losses
- why a star-delta starter is used for induction motors

"Why is a transformer rated in kVA?" has **one** candidate report (Siemens Energy, via snippet).

### Inferences
- A defensible "most frequently reported" label applies at the **topic** level to **transformers** and **induction motors** (7+ employers each). At the specific-question level, the label only fits "explain the working / types of an induction motor" (3 sources), and even then the wording differs across sources.
- "Why is a transformer rated in kVA?" is almost certainly common in practice: it appears in nearly every question bank, and in one Siemens Energy candidate report. **But this research could only confirm 1 candidate report.** Mark it "Reported in interview (1 source)" rather than "most frequent".
- Power-systems-specific questions (Ferranti, corona, skin effect, fault severity, per-unit) did **not** appear in any candidate account found. Interviewers at these companies seem to lean on machines more than on transmission-line theory, but that may be a visibility bias of the snippets.

### Gaps
- **Glassdoor, AmbitionBox and Quora returned 403**, so the full review text (exact wording, follow-ups, candidate's college and year, fresher status) could not be read. A browser-based pass over those pages would likely add many more data points.
- **No Reddit evidence at all:** searches for r/ElectricalEngineering, r/Indian_Academia, r/btech and r/IndianEngineers threads with first-person core-EE questions surfaced nothing.
- **No LinkedIn posts or YouTube descriptions** were examined (none surfaced in search).
- **PSU interviews** (NTPC, POWERGRID, IOCL) are under-sampled. The only NTPC source is a 2011 blog that was not fetched.

## What typical follow-up questions do interviewers ask after the first answer?

### Takeaway
No first-person source in this research recorded an interviewer's follow-up chain for a machines or power-systems question. The pairing "transformer in kVA → then why are motors rated in kW?" exists, but only as a **question-bank / Q&A pairing**, not as a reported follow-up.

### Cited Findings
- **Emami GET 2023:** the account lists 5 technical questions with **no follow-ups** recorded ([GfG](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/?p=1074649)).
- **kVA vs kW pairing:** a Careers360 Q&A page pairs the two in one question, "Why transformer rating is in kVA while motor rating is in kW?" ([Careers360](https://www.careers360.com/question-why-transformer-rating-is-in-kva-while-motor-rating-is-in-kw)). A question bank gives the paired reasoning: a transformer's power factor depends on the connected load, while a motor's power factor depends on its construction ([eeemadeeasy](https://www.eeemadeeasy.com/electrical-engineering-interview-questions-answers/)). Status: Preparation list.
- **Star-delta follow-up material (prep sources):** star-delta reduces starting current (the "√3 / 3 times" figures quoted vary by source). The natural follow-up is the disadvantage: starting torque falls, because torque is proportional to V² ([electricalvolt induction motor Q51-60](https://www.electricalvolt.com/induction-motor-interview-questions-and-answers-q-51-60/)). Status: Preparation list.
- **Single-phase induction motor:** prep sources pair "why is a 1-phase induction motor not self-starting" (at standstill the two field components give equal and opposite torques, so net torque is zero) with "how is it made self-starting" (an auxiliary winding to create a rotating field) ([inductionmotorsinterviewquestions blog](https://inductionmotorsinterviewquestions.blogspot.com/2017/09/single-phase-induction-motors-interview-questions.html)). The ReNew candidate was asked how a single-phase induction motor works ([Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Interview/ReNew-Interview-E675767-RVW75863135.htm)), but the snippet shows no follow-up. Status: first question Reported (1 source); follow-up Preparation list.
- **"What happens when a transformer is energized"** (Tata Power, [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com.hk/Interview/Tata-Power-Interview-Questions-E152741_P5.htm)) naturally leads to inrush current / no-load current. No source confirms the interviewer followed up.

### Inferences
These follow-up chains are textbook-logical and well suited to the mock interviewer's "dig_deeper" move. They should be labelled **"typical follow-up (preparation)"**, not "reported":
- kVA → motors in kW
- star-delta → torque penalty → why not DOL for large motors
- 1-phase not self-starting → capacitor-start
- transformer energized → inrush

### Gaps
- No candidate account with a recorded follow-up sequence was readable. Full Glassdoor/AmbitionBox review text (blocked) or GfG EE interview experiences (only one found) would be needed.

## Are there questions that are widely recommended but with NO evidence of being asked? Mark them as preparation-only.

- Ferranti effect, corona, skin effect
- most severe fault
- circuit breaker vs isolator vs fuse

- per-unit system
- OC/SC tests
- why not DC on a transformer
- tap changers
- alternator synchronisation, V-curves, why alternators are rated in kVA
- why 3-phase motors self-start

Mark all of these **Preparation list**. That does not mean they are never asked; it means no candidate report was found here.

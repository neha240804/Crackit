## Which questions in these subjects did candidates report being asked (company, role, year)?

### Takeaway
Few first-hand reports name the exact questions. No reported fresher question on control systems (PID, stability) was found, and none on network-theorem calculations beyond Thevenin.

## Which numerical questions are reported as asked in interviews (not just exam problems)?

### Takeaway
**No source found reports a specific numerical (with values) asked in an Indian EE fresher interview.** Searches for transformer turns-ratio, synchronous speed/slip and similar interview numericals returned only GATE question banks and formula references. Reported interviews instead ask the **concepts behind** the formulas (per-unit "why", kVA rating, starting current, RC waveform).

### Inferences
- For the product's question bank, numericals (turns ratio/current, Ns and slip, per-unit conversion, PF-correction capacitor, star-delta conversion, resonance frequency f = 1/(2π√LC), RC time constant τ = RC) should be labelled **"Preparation list (interview-style numerical)"**, not "Reported", unless better sources are found. Difficulty: Hard.
- Natural follow-ups after a numerical (inferred): Ns/slip → why the rotor never reaches Ns; turns ratio → current ratio inverse, so the HV side has thinner wire; PF capacitor → what happens with over-correction; RC τ → voltage after 1τ (≈63%) and 5τ (≈fully charged).

## Which practical/industrial questions recur (e.g. "why is the neutral earthed?", "what happens if CT secondary is opened?")?

### Takeaway
The **CT secondary open-circuit** question is the most consistently recurring practical question. It shows up as an AmbitionBox interview-question entry, in a Glassdoor CT/PT listing, and in several PSU-prep lists. Earthing (need for it, how to measure it, earthing vs grounding, neutral vs body earthing) and **MCB/MCCB/ELCB** follow closely, then transformer maintenance/protection and motor starting current.

### Cited Findings
- CT secondary: AmbitionBox entry "Why is the CT secondary side shorted?" (answer: open secondary can develop dangerous induced voltage) — [AmbitionBox](https://www.ambitionbox.com/interviews/question/why-is-the-ct-secondary-site-shorted-eM2yAyPf); a prep blog answer says opening the CT circuit saturates the core and can damage it — [Technocracy IQ](https://technocracyiq.blogspot.com/2017/06/electrical-engineering-technical-q-part_15.html); listed as a PSU-interview prep question — [BYJU'S Exam Prep](https://byjusexamprep.com/electrical-interview-questions-i) (search snippet; fetch returned an empty page).
- Neutral earthing (Preparation list): with an earthed neutral, earth-fault current flows through the neutral and is sensed by a CT and relay so the fault is cleared quickly — [Technocracy IQ](https://technocracyiq.blogspot.com/2017/06/electrical-engineering-technical-q-part_15.html).
- Earthing recurring: "Why earthing is necessary for all electrical equipments?" — [AmbitionBox EE](https://ambitionbox.com/profiles/electrical-engineer/interview-questions); earthing test instrument and acceptable value (electrician role) — [AmbitionBox Electrician](https://ambitionbox.com/profiles/electrician/interview-questions); earthing vs grounding (prep) — [UPES](https://www.upes.ac.in/blog/advanced-engineering/electrical-engineering-interview-questions).
- Breakers: "What are MCB, MCCB, and ELCB?" — [AmbitionBox](https://www.ambitionbox.com/interviews/question/what-are-mcb-mccb-and-elcb-cAQlXvUh); single-pole vs double-pole breaker (Bajaj, 2019) — [GeeksforGeeks](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/?p=357010); repeated MCB/relay tripping troubleshooting (prep) — [UPES](https://www.upes.ac.in/blog/advanced-engineering/electrical-engineering-interview-questions).

### Inferences
- A good practical cluster for the mock interviewer: CT open secondary → PT fuse → neutral vs body earthing → earth-resistance testing (instrument) → MCB/MCCB/ELCB/RCCB → MCB nuisance tripping troubleshooting. Each step is backed by at least one reported or prep source above.
- Expected follow-ups (inferred): after CT open-circuit, "What do you do before removing a meter from a live CT circuit?" (short the secondary); after earthing, "What earth-resistance value is acceptable and which instrument measures it?" (the reported electrician question).

### Gaps

- No reported fresher question on substations (layout, equipment sequence) or ELCB vs RCCB was found.
- Recurrence is judged from how many sources list a question, not from any frequency statistics. None exist in the sources found.
